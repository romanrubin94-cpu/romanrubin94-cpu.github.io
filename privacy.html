ACC Gmail - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Who uses this application

ACC Gmail is a private, personal assistant application. It is used by exactly one person, its owner Roman Rubin, with his own Google account, on his own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

  • gmail.readonly - to read the owner's own email messages, so the assistant can find and summarize important mail for him.
  • gmail.send - to send email from the owner's own account, only after the owner explicitly confirms each message.

The application does not delete, modify or label email, and does not access contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

Email data is used only to provide the owner with summaries, translations and replies that he requests. Access tokens and any copies of message data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Parts of message content may be processed by AI services that the owner uses under his own accounts, only to complete a task he asked for.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, never shared with third parties for advertising, and never used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models by this application.

ACC Gmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local tokens and data can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.
